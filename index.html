Meet Mariette

Hair styling with skill, care, and culture.

Mariette Bondey specializes in African hair styling and protective styles. Her work combines technical skill, creativity, and attention to the individual needs of each client.

Her goal is not only to create beautiful hair, but also to improve the full salon experience by understanding customer needs, reducing common pain points, and making appointments more comfortable, transparent, and user-centered.

Client Experience

Designed around customer needs and pain points.

The salon experience should be as thoughtful as the finished hairstyle.

?

Clear Expectations

Pain point: Clients may not know what service to select, how long it will take, or what hair to bring.

Improvement: Simple service descriptions, preparation instructions, and consultation support.

⏱

Respect for Time

Pain point: Long or unpredictable wait times can make salon visits stressful.

Improvement: Appointment-based scheduling, estimated service duration, and clear communication.

♡

Comfort & Care

Pain point: Tight styles, uncomfortable seating, or lack of check-ins can reduce trust.

Improvement: Comfort check-ins, gentle handling, and a welcoming environment.

$

Transparent Pricing

Pain point: Clients can feel uncertain when prices or add-on costs are unclear.

Improvement: Upfront pricing ranges and confirmation before additional services.
